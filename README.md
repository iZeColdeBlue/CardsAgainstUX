# Cards Against UX

A party card game about the everyday joys and pains of UX work, made by the [UX Graz](https://www.meetup.com/uxgraz) community.

This is an open remix of **Cards Against Humanity**. Same game, new cards: a bunch of prompts and answers about research, stakeholders, design systems and everything in between.

## Who made this

The idea came from the UX Graz organizers, Johannes, Ani, Kornelia and me (Jakob). We sat together, came up with the first batch of cards, and then ran a meetup where everyone could add their own. So this deck belongs to the whole UX Graz community. Thanks to everyone who contributed a card (or a very questionable joke).

## Print it

1. Download the PDF from the [latest release](../../releases/latest).
2. Print on thick paper or card stock (A4). Prompt cards are black, answer cards are white.
3. Cut along the lines.
4. Find some friends who have survived at least one design review.

## How to play

- Everyone draws 7 white answer cards.
- The person who most recently sat through a usability test is the first **Card Czar**. They draw a black prompt card and read it out loud.
- Everyone else picks the white card from their hand that fits best (or worst) and hands it to the Card Czar face down. If the prompt asks for 2 cards, play two cards.
- The Card Czar shuffles the answers, reads each one out together with the prompt, and picks a favourite.
- Whoever played the winning card keeps the black card as a point.
- Everyone draws back up to 7 cards, and the Card Czar role moves to the next person on the left.
- Play until you get bored or the pizza arrives. Most black cards wins.

House rules are very welcome. Make up your own.
